The Q1 2026 earnings call for FIG Group revealed a massive pivot in their business model. For the first time, revenue from tokenized asset services has outpaced traditional brokerage fees.

### Key Highlights
* **Total Assets under Management (AUM):** $1.2B on-chain.
* **Fee Revenue:** Increased by 40% compared to Q1 2025.
* **Future Outlook:** Planning to expand into tokenized carbon credits by Q3.

This report confirms that RWA is no longer a "side project" for traditional finance firms; it is becoming a core revenue driver.
