Plume Network is emerging as a critical player in the "RWA Summer" narrative. By creating a modular Layer 2 specifically for tokenized assets, they are removing the compliance and technical hurdles that have historically slowed down adoption.

### Why Plume Matters
Most blockchains are general-purpose. Plume, however, integrates compliance, identity (KYC/KYB), and specialized RWA oracles directly into the chain's architecture.

### Early Traction
With over 100 projects in their ecosystem, Plume is positioning itself as the 'App Store' for tokenized yield-bearing assets.
