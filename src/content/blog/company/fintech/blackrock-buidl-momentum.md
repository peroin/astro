BlackRock has officially set the gold standard for institutional RWA integration. Since the launch of the **BUIDL** fund on Ethereum, the conversation has shifted from "if" institutions will adopt DeFi to "how fast" they can migrate.

### The Strategic Moat
Unlike previous attempts at tokenization, BUIDL provides 24/7/365 yield generation and instant settlement. This isn't just a product; it's a structural upgrade to how treasury management works in the digital age.

### Market Impact
The fund has already attracted over $500 million in assets, proving that capital is ready to move on-chain if the provider is trusted.
